# Mission Reflection – Multi-Cloud Explorer

Completing this laboratory helped me understand the differences and similarities between AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. Among the three cloud platforms, Google Cloud impressed me the most because of its strong capabilities in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. I also learned that AWS and Azure have many powerful services that can support different types of organizations and workloads.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide cloud computing services such as virtual machines, storage, networking, databases, identity management, and Kubernetes. They also allow organizations to scale resources according to their needs instead of relying only on physical servers. Although their services have different names, many of them provide similar functions.

Business requirements are very important when selecting a cloud platform. For example, a company that relies heavily on Microsoft technologies may benefit from Azure, while an AI research company may prefer Google Cloud. A startup or global e-commerce company may choose AWS because of its wide range of services and scalability.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I need to understand the client's budget, existing technologies, workload requirements, scalability needs, and business goals before making a recommendation. Comparing equivalent services across the three providers also helped me understand how different cloud platforms can solve similar problems.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has improved because I now have organized documentation, cloud platform comparisons, client recommendations, screenshots, and Linux investigation results. This laboratory also improved my Markdown and GitHub skills. Overall, the mission helped me develop a better understanding of multi-cloud environments and how technical decisions should be connected to business requirements.
